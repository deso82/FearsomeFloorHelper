Monster (Furunkulus) Movement Algorithm

Overview

Furunkulus moves after all players have moved their tokens each round. A monster movement card is drawn to determine how far (or how many victims) the monster pursues.

Step-by-Step Algorithm

1

Draw Monster Card

Flip the top card of the monster deck. Number = steps to move. Cross (†) = move until eating that many players (max 20 steps).

2

Look Ahead, Left & Right

Before each step, the monster scans three directions: straight ahead, left, and right (never behind). It looks along each direction until vision is blocked by a stone or the board edge.

Crystal stones do NOT block line of sight. Teleporters are flat and don't block sight either.
3

Choose Direction

  • No tokens visible: Continue straight ahead.
  • One closest token: Turn toward it and move one step.
  • Multiple tokens, one closest: Turn toward the closest one.
  • Tie (equidistant tokens): Monster is confused — continues straight ahead.
4

Move One Space

The monster advances one space in its chosen direction. If it enters a space with a player token, that player is eaten.

5

Repeat

Go back to step 2 for each remaining movement point. The monster re-evaluates its line of sight on every single space it enters.

Special Interactions

■

Stones

Block line of sight and movement. Monster pushes stones (and anything behind them). Stones pushed off the board are removed. Players crushed between a stone and a wall are eaten.

◆

Crystal Stones

Block movement like normal stones but do NOT block line of sight. Monster can see through them but still pushes them.

↷

Right Turning Stone

Cannot be pushed by the monster. When monster enters the space, it first looks left & right. If no player visible, the turning stone forces a right turn. Then the monster moves one step.

↺

180° Turning Stone

Cannot be pushed. Works like right turning stone but forces a 180° reversal. Monster looks left & right first; only activates if no player visible.

◼

Blood Pool

Immovable. Any token (player or monster) entering slides straight across. Monster cannot look left/right while sliding through blood.

☍

Teleporter

Flat (doesn't block sight). Monster stepping on a teleporter is instantly transported to the matching teleporter, facing the arrow direction. It then looks left & right before continuing.

↔

Walls (Board Edge)

When the monster moves off the board, it wraps around to the space with the matching letter on the opposite wall, maintaining direction.

Complete Rules Reference

Game Overview

Fearsome Floor (Finstere Flure) is a race game for 2–7 players by Friedemann Friese. Players guide their tokens through a dungeon to the exit while avoiding the monster Furunkulus. The game lasts a maximum of 14 turns.

  • Furunkulus starts at the top-left exit space, facing toward the "M" on the opposite wall.
  • First turn: each player moves only 2 of their tokens onto the board.

  • Each token has two sides with numbers that add up to 7.
  • Move up to the number shown on the currently visible face.
  • After moving, flip the token to show the other side.
  • Movement is orthogonal (horizontal/vertical), not diagonal.
  • May move through other player tokens (counts as spaces).
  • Must end on an empty space.
  • May choose not to move (just flip the token).
  • Cannot move through stones or off the board edges.
  • Can push a stone if the space behind it is empty.

Turn Structure

  1. Players move tokens in clockwise order. Each player moves all their tokens (one at a time, in any order), flipping each after moving.
  2. Monster moves after all players are done. Draw monster card and execute movement.
  3. Pass start player marker clockwise.

Exiting the Dungeon

The exit is at the top-left corner. To leave, a token needs one additional movement point after reaching the corner exit space. Tokens that exit are safe and score toward the win condition.

Winning the Game

  • 2–4 players: First to get 3 of 4 tokens out wins.
  • 5–7 players: First to get 2 of 3 tokens out wins.
  • If no one wins by end of turn 14, the dungeon collapses — everyone loses!

Strategy Tips

  • Hide behind stones to block the monster's line of sight.
  • Bait your opponents — position so the monster is drawn toward them.
  • Watch equidistance — if two tokens are equally far, the monster goes straight. Use this to your advantage.
  • Plan 2 turns ahead — remember your token flips (3 this turn means 4 next turn).
  • Use blood pools to slide past dangerous areas quickly.
  • Push stones strategically to create new hiding spots or block the monster's path.
  • Turning stones are powerful tools to redirect the monster toward opponents.
